Accepted for Publication • AKGEC 2026

Automatic Mixing Process Plant Using PLC, HMI and SCADA for Industry 4.0 Automation

Authors: Abhay Gahlot et al.
Conference Dates: 26–28 February 2026
Venue: Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College, Ghaziabad

Abstract: This research paper presents the design, development, and implementation of an automated industrial batch mixing process plant leveraging programmable logic controller (PLC) sequencing, human-machine interface (HMI), and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) telemetry. The proposed architecture eliminates operator manual handling variance, guarantees reproducible batch proportions, incorporates fail-safe interlocks, and provides real-time diagnostic event logging aligned with modern Industry 4.0 manufacturing paradigms.
